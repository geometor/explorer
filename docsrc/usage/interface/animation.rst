animation timeline
==================

The interactive timeline panel visualizes geometric constructions as step-by-step sequential animations.

Timeline Bar Controls
---------------------

Click the movie icon (`movie`) in the left toolbar to toggle the bottom animation timeline bar:

- **Play / Pause**: Start or pause real-time sequence playback.
- **Scrubbable Slider**: Drag the timeline handle to jump to any point step in the construction history.
- **Start (Skip Previous)**: Jump to step 0 (initial given points).
- **Step Backward (Fast Rewind)**: Step back one construction step.
- **Step Forward (Fast Forward)**: Advance forward one construction step.
- **End (Skip Next)**: Advance directly to the final state of the model.

Keyboard Controls
-----------------

When the canvas is active, use the left and right arrow keys to step backward and forward through the construction sequence.
